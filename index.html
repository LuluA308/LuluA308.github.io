Lulu Abderrazeq

Pre-Dental | Psychology Student🦷

Hi, I'm Lulu! I'm a psychology student at the University of California, Riverside, on the pre-dental track, and my goal is to become a dentist. I graduated as valedictorian of Universal Civilizations Academy in May 2024 with a 4.0 GPA, then completed 62 college credits at Chaffey College before transferring to UC Riverside in Fall 2026. My interest in dentistry grew out of two things I care about: science and people. In my coursework, including General Chemistry I and II, Physics, and Calculus I, I've built the scientific foundation that dental school requires. At the same time, my psychology major has taught me how people think, how they learn, and how they handle stress and anxiety. I believe that understanding the patient is just as important as understanding the procedure, and I want to be the kind of provider who makes nervous patients feel heard, comfortable, and cared for.



Outside the classroom, I've spent time serving my community. I organized a fundraising event that raised $3,000 for breast cancer, which taught me how to plan, delegate, communicate, and follow through on a goal that matters. I have also provided after-school tutoring and academic support to children with special educational needs, helping them with their schoolwork while adapting to each child's individual learning needs. Working with them taught me patience and creativity, and it showed me how important it is to meet each person where they are instead of using a one-size-fits-all approach. That is a lesson I plan to carry into my future patient care.



Right now, I'm looking for opportunities to shadow dentists, observe procedures, and gain hands-on experience in a dental office. I want to learn about the daily work of dentistry, from patient communication to clinical techniques and the teamwork behind a successful practice. My strengths are communication, organization and time management, and attention to detail, and I'm excited to keep learning, keep serving, and keep working toward dental school. If you're a dentist or work in the field and would be open to a conversation or a shadowing opportunity, I would love to hear from you. Feel free to reach out using the links below.
